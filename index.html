Services

From first idea to show day

Design and production support for artists, management and production teams working on concerts and tours.

01

Lighting design

Lighting rigs, looks and cue structure designed around the artist, the music and the room, from a single concert hall show to a full arena tour.

02

Show & production design

A complete visual concept that brings lighting, video and staging together, developed with content studios and suppliers so every element works as one show.

03

Pre-visualisation

Lighting plots drawn in wysiwyg and full shows pre-visualised in Depence², so looks can be built, reviewed and agreed before load-in.

04

Programming & operation

Show programming and operation on MA Lighting grandMA2 and grandMA3 consoles, from rehearsals through to show day.

About

About Edd Croft Ltd

Edd Croft Ltd was set up in Manchester in March 2022 as the company of lighting and show designer Edd Croft. It provides support services to the performing arts, with a focus on concerts and touring productions.

Edd is part of Mångata Collective, a design and production team that takes shows from the creative concept and visual design through to production, with pre-visualisation at its studio in Stockport. Each show is shaped together with artists, management, content studios and suppliers, so that lighting and video read as one.
